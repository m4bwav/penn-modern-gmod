# LIRR concourse dimensions (area 2)

Cited (Wikipedia, "New York Penn Station", read 2026-11-26, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Penn_Station):
- 2023 rebuild: the corridor was doubled in width and the ceilings raised to at least 18 ft by removing seven 6 ft 8 in "head knockers" (original Penn Station beams).
- A new 33rd Street entrance (entry pavilion) opened with it.

Derived (judged from the refs in refs/photos/, logged in refs/SOURCES.md):
- Width 57 ft (an old ~28 ft corridor doubled). Ceiling 18 ft.
- Two ceilings either side of a column row: warm wood slats with lit lines on one side, white panels on the other (Penn_Station_LIRR_concourse_renovated_high_ceiling.jpg). Columns ~2.5 ft square every ~30 ft, white fading to deep blue at the base.
- 33rd Street pavilion: a lit blue coffer grid (~10 ft coffers) about 40 ft up, escalators rising to street level, shops on one side, an overhead band of wayfinding signs (LIRR_Penn_Station_2025a.jpg).
- Passage under Eighth Avenue from the West End Concourse: 30 ft wide, 12 ft high, ~90 ft long. A simplification: the real route runs through the West End Concourse and the Amtrak/LIRR levels; areas 3 and 4 will hang off this corridor.
