# Amtrak concourse dimensions (area 3)

Cited (Wikipedia, "New York Penn Station", Station layout, read 2026-11-27, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Penn_Station):
- The Amtrak and NJ Transit concourses are on the first level below the street; the LIRR concourse is two levels below.
- The main concourse, used mainly by Amtrak until Moynihan Train Hall opened in 2021, is at the west end of the block beneath Madison Square Garden and is now used mainly by NJ Transit. It holds an enclosed ticketed waiting area, renovated 2019-2020 with an LED-lit ceiling.

Derived (judged from the refs in refs/photos/, logged in refs/SOURCES.md):
- Floor 20 ft below the street, 10 ft above the LIRR floor (10 risers of 12 in). Ceiling 14 ft, charcoal louvres with square downlights (Amtrak_terminal_New_York_Penn_December_27_2022.jpg); a raised white centre over the escalator atrium, 18 ft.
- Hall 240 x 150 ft, south of the LIRR corridor. Round blue columns ~3 ft across on a ~30 ft grid, each with a ring of lights under the ceiling (NY_Penn_Station_Amtrak_Terminal.jpg, Penn_Station_concourse.jpg).
- A 40 x 10 ft departures board on the north wall (Penn_Station_NYC_Amtrak_departure_board_14086237286.jpg); ticket counters with glass screens on the west wall; a glazed ticketed waiting room with rows of black seats (Amtrak_Waiting_Room_Penn_Station_NY_during_COVID_Pandemic.jpg); shops along the south wall.
- Simplifications: one 16 ft stair from the LIRR corridor (the real station has several stairs and escalators between the levels); one escalator well toward the tracks ending at a sealed landing 30 ft down until area 5 adds the platforms.
