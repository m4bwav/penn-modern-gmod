# Platform dimensions (area 5)

Cited:
- Wikipedia, "New York Penn Station" (read 2026-11-27, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Penn_Station): 21 tracks and 11 platforms below the concourses; tracks run east-west under the block.
- Wikipedia, "Bombardier MultiLevel Coach" (read 2026-11-27): about 85 ft long, 10 ft 6 in wide, 15 ft 6 in tall.
- Standard gauge 4 ft 8.5 in (56.5 in). High-level platforms on the Northeast Corridor stand 48 in above top of rail.
